Professional profile

My training combines astrophysics with electronics and instrumentation, which means I naturally end up asking not only what the universe is doing, but also how well our instruments are measuring it. Much of my work has therefore focused on precision: how optical systems respond to their environment, how detector-plane motion can be stabilised, and how feedback logic can improve the reliability of measurements.

My broader interests include exoplanet instrumentation, radial-velocity spectroscopy, optical path stability, brown dwarfs, detector performance, and scientific computing for experimental systems. I enjoy technical work that sits between physics and engineering rather than pretending the two should stay in separate rooms.

Research focus

My research interests sit around the practical side of astrophysics: what it takes to build, stabilise, and interpret instruments that are supposed to measure very small effects without being distracted by everything else the environment is doing.

Selected experience

EXOhSPEC closed-loop feedback control project

Worked on stabilisation ideas for a precision spectrograph platform, focusing on environmental response, optical path behaviour, detector-plane stability, and control logic tied to temperature and image motion.

SEPnet placement with Lumi Space

Contributed to detector-performance analysis relevant to satellite laser ranging, combining practical measurement work with instrument-oriented interpretation.

Halo T dwarf candidate research

Worked with VISTA, DES, and WISE data for the study of candidate T dwarfs, linking survey-based astrophysics with classification and follow-up motivation.

Radio interferometry summer school project

Participated in hands-on interferometry training, strengthening my interest in observational methods and data-driven astronomical inference.

Visible light communication system using Raspberry Pi

Designed and implemented a VLC system with dimming control, bringing together hardware, software, optical communication, and experimental testing.
