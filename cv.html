02

Research & Project Experience

2023–Present
Closed-Loop Feedback Control System for EXOhSPEC
MSc research project · University of Hertfordshire
Supervisors: Prof. Hugh Jones & Prof. William Martin
  • Developed a closed-loop stabilisation framework for EXOhSPEC, a high-resolution radial-velocity spectrograph testbed for exoplanet instrumentation research.
  • Integrated IDS3010 interferometric sensing, Meerstetter TEC control, BME environmental sensors, MaxIm DL centroid tracking, and adaptive-optics correction into Python-based feedback workflows.
  • Quantified environmental coupling: 1 hPa pressure → ~0.4 µm OPL; 1°C temperature → ~6 µm OPL variation.
  • Achieved ~±0.3°C enclosure stability over multi-week operation, later reaching millikelvin-level TEC control during closed-loop experiments.
  • Built diagnostics stack: Grafana + InfluxDB, serial logging, web monitoring, and Python-based OPL and pixel-drift correction models using environmental data and guider centroid errors.
  • AO Stage-1 tests validated ~0.15 px/step response; reduced mean post-correction residuals from ~0.44 px to ~0.15 px; hybrid TEC–AO runs reduced dY RMS from ~2.72 px to ~0.69 px.
