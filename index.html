About

Between physics, engineering and measurement

Two disciplines, one workflow — I sit where the hardware ends and the data begins.

My BTech in Electronics and Communication Engineering is the practical half of how I work: building and wiring hardware, reading sensors, handling embedded systems, and taming noisy signals before they ever become data.

My MSc in Astrophysics with Advanced Research (University of Hertfordshire) is the other half: framing the measurement problem, analysing the data, and interpreting what the numbers actually mean physically.

In practice the two meet constantly. I'm drawn to problems where the result depends on getting both the instrument and the analysis right, and I work iteratively — build, measure, diagnose, refine.

Python · scientific data analysis · feedback control · adaptive optics · thermal control (TEC) · laser interferometry · electronics & embedded systems · LaTeX · Grafana / InfluxDB

MSc research contribution to supervised spectrograph feedback-control work

As part of my MSc research at the University of Hertfordshire, I contributed to supervised work on closed-loop feedback control for the EXOhSPEC high-resolution spectrograph project, with emphasis on instrumentation, sensing, experimental logging and control-system analysis.

Supervised by Prof. Hugh Jones and Prof. Bill Martin.
