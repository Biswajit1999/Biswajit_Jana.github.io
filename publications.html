Journal publications

The publication list below reflects the route I took into astrophysics: beginning from electronics and optical communication, then moving toward instrumentation and precision measurement in astronomy.

Journal Publication

Implementation of dimming-controlled visible light communication using Raspberry Pi

Optical and Quantum Electronics · Springer · 2021

This work combines optical communication, Raspberry Pi implementation, control of illumination level, and experimental testing. It remains a useful example of how my engineering background developed through practical systems rather than abstract enthusiasm alone.

DOI: 10.1007/s11082-021-03362-4

Conference proceedings

Conference Proceeding

GSM controlled location-specific garbage collecting smart bin

IEMECON · Jaipur, India · 2019

A systems-oriented project focused on sensing, automation, and communication. The paper reflects an early interest in device behaviour, system logic, and practical implementation.

DOI: 10.1109/IEMECONX.2019.8877007

Conference Proceeding

Priority encoder using reversible logic gates in QCA

IEEE IEMCON · Vancouver, Canada · 2017

An earlier logic-design contribution, focused on reversible logic and QCA implementation. It sits far from astronomy in subject, but not far from my long-term habit of enjoying technical problems more than is probably healthy.

DOI: 10.1109/IEMCON.2017.8117242
