# EXOhSPEC MSc research archive

## [Open the public academic archive →](https://biswajit1999.github.io/Master-Thesis-2024/)

This repository preserves a selected public record of the MSc research undertaken by **Biswajit Jana** at the **University of Hertfordshire** during 2023–2024.

Public material retained here:

- the submitted MSc thesis;
- the EXOhSPEC research poster;
- the MSc project presentation; and
- seminar presentations 1–4.

Stable filenames are retained so that links in CVs, portfolios and applications continue to resolve.

## Research context

**Project:** Closed-Loop Feedback Control System for EXOhSPEC Development

**Author:** Biswajit Jana

**Supervisors:** Prof Hugh R. A. Jones and Prof William E. Martin

**Institution:** University of Hertfordshire, United Kingdom

## Access boundary

This public repository does not contain post-thesis experimental logs, raw measurements, current working reports, private correspondence or unpublished results. Those records remain in a controlled workspace.

Authorised collaborators can use the [EXOhSPEC member portal](https://exohspec-member-research-2026.web.app/) to sign in. Accounts other than the research owner require individual approval.

For the wider instrument programme, see the [official EXOhSPEC website](https://star.herts.ac.uk/exohspec/).
