Virtual Spectrographic Laboratory

A one-file black-hole simulator upgraded into an observing lab: Schwarzschild-style ray-bending, Doppler-beamed accretion disk colour, ACES filmic tonemapping, a draggable spectrograph slit, and a live synthetic Iron K-alpha line extracted from the pixels under the slit.
