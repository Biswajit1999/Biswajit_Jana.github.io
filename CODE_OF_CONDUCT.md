# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This repository supports respectful discussion of an academic portfolio, scientific communication, and educational web tools. Participants are expected to be constructive, precise, and considerate.

Examples of acceptable participation include:

- respectfully identifying a website problem or a factual correction;
- giving clear, evidence-based technical or scientific feedback; and
- respecting copyright, attribution, privacy, and research context.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or insulting comments, deliberate misinformation, unsolicited promotion, disclosure of private information, or requests to misuse or republish protected site material.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, discussions, or other contributions that do not meet this standard, and may restrict participation where necessary.

## Reporting

Conduct concerns may be reported privately through the contact information on the [published website](https://biswajit1999.github.io/Biswajit_Jana.github.io/#contact). Security vulnerabilities must instead be reported according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
